Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

This privacy policy covers the Facebook app named my app and the related software published by its owner (github.com/sudip490). The app is a tool used by the owner to manage the owner's own Facebook Page, including publishing videos and reels to that Page.

What information is used

The app uses the access tokens that Facebook provides after the owner signs in, only to publish content to the owner's own Facebook Page and to read that Page's basic information and post status. It does not collect personal information from visitors, followers, or other Facebook users.

How information is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer or server, are used only to talk to Facebook's API, and are never sold, shared, or sent to third parties.

Cookies and tracking

The app does not use cookies and does not track anyone.

Third parties

The app communicates only with Facebook (Meta Platforms) through its official API, under Facebook's own privacy policy.

Data deletion

If you believe the app holds any data about you and want it removed, contact the owner through github.com/sudip490. Any such data will be deleted promptly. You can also remove the app's access at any time from your Facebook settings under "Apps and Websites".

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address.
